# Janna AI identity

Janna AI is the product brand for this repository, by Jepong Devxyz. The current
mark is a simple geometric "J" monogram (indigo with a rose accent dot) in
`web/mark-clean.png`, `web/mark.png`, `web/icon-192.png`, `web/icon-512.png` and
`assets/janna-icon*.png` / `assets/janna.ico`.

## Upstream attribution

Janna AI is an independent rebrand of the open-source
[Takumi](https://github.com/coye2/Takumi) workspace by Coye, used under the MIT
License (see `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY.md`). The original Takumi logo and
master artwork are **not** used as the Janna AI brand and are not claimed as
Janna AI artwork.

## Kage companions

The Kage companion characters (Ren, Kiri, Hana, Tetsu, Akane) originate from the
upstream Takumi project and are retained as app features with credit to the
upstream project. They are not Janna AI original artwork.

## Model identity

**Sumi 1.1** and **Rei 1.1** are the local model profile names inherited from
upstream. They are inference configurations over separately downloaded Bonsai
models, not newly trained foundation models. Their upstream attribution is
retained in `THIRD_PARTY.md` and `models/`.
