# Janna AI identity

Janna AI is the product brand for this repository, by Jepong Devxyz. The current
mark is a simple geometric "J" monogram (indigo with a rose accent dot) in
`web/mark-clean.png`, `web/mark.png`, `web/icon-192.png`, `web/icon-512.png` and
`assets/janna-icon*.png` / `assets/janna.ico`.

## Luna companion

Luna, the little indigo star cat, is Janna AI's pet companion — original
artwork created for Janna AI. She lives in the companion panel and the
floating pet button, reacts to work, and chats in her own persona.

## Model identity

**Janna Pro 1.1** and **Janna Lite 1.1** are Janna AI's local model profiles.
They are inference configurations over separately downloaded Bonsai models,
not newly trained foundation models. Base-model notices live in
`THIRD_PARTY.md` and `models/`.
